Filters

The members of the clumpfilter type the time step fills, where they are built and under which conditions, and what follows for pairs of them.

  • disjoint (⊥): no element in common, from the two branches of one if or a curated fact about landunit types.
  • subset (⊂): built inside a loop over the other, or under its conditions and more.
  • Pairs with no derived relation are assumed to overlap. The tree graph drops a variable from a dependency only when every write and every read of it run under filters known to be disjoint.
