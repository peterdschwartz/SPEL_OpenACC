The time step as a tree

Each box is one call made by the root routine under the active configuration. A line runs from a routine to a later one that reads what it wrote.

  • A routine sits one row below the last routine it depends on; routines with no line between them are independent. Within a row, left to right is call order.
  • A blue outline and › mark a routine with callees of its own: click it to open them. Grey-outlined boxes are leaves. Double-click any box for the routine page, hover a line for the shared variables.
  • Book-keeping routines (summaries, zeroing, balance checks, budgets, accumulated variables, bounds, stream interpolation) are hidden unless asked for; rows and lines are recomputed from the routines shown.
  • With "show pruned", routines the configuration never reaches appear grey-filled and labelled pruned; dashed boxes were not analyzed, ↺ marks a recursive call. Scroll to move, drag or ctrl+wheel to zoom.
